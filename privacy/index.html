Your privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Divine Lines is a Christian scripture and devotional wallpaper app. This policy explains how the app and this support website handle information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your name or profile preferences, faith and topic choices, bookmarks, passage history, theme selections, and reading interval settings locally on your device. Any notes or reflections you save are also local. You do not need a developer account, email address, or social login to read scripture or save bookmarks.

We do not operate a remote profile database for these reading preferences. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple or Google backup settings. Local app storage is not a separate encrypted vault.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. RevenueCat helps the app retrieve subscription offerings, validate purchases, restore purchases, and determine premium access. The app connects to RevenueCat when its purchase service initializes, even if you do not buy a subscription.

RevenueCat may process a pseudonymous app user identifier, purchase receipts and transaction information, subscription status, and device or app information needed to provide its service. Network providers also receive connection information such as your IP address. We do not receive your full payment card or bank details. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy, Apple’s privacy policy, and Google’s privacy policy.

Photos, wallpapers, widgets, and sharing

When you choose to save an image, the app requests the relevant device permission to export the wallpaper to your photo library. It does not use this feature to upload your photo library. On supported Android devices, wallpaper actions use the system wallpaper service. On iOS, selected content may be shared locally with the app’s widget through an App Group.

Sharing a verse or image opens your device’s share controls. The service you choose then handles the shared content under its own policies.

Analytics and advertising

The current app does not include advertising, behavioral tracking, or a remote analytics or crash-reporting service. We do not sell your personal information or send your bookmarks, religious preferences, or reflections to advertising networks. Purchase processing described above still applies.

Support and your choices

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to answer your request and resolve support issues. Please do not send payment card details or sensitive personal reflections.

You can remove bookmarks in the app and manage device permissions in system settings. Clearing local app data or deleting the app removes its local data, subject to device backup behavior. This does not cancel a subscription, delete exported images, or erase records held by Apple, Google, or RevenueCat.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information we hold, contact hello.toolsmint@gmail.com. We may need enough information to identify the relevant support or purchase record. Some transaction records may need to be retained for legal or operational reasons.

Children

The app is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

This website and policy updates

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or contact forms. Brand fonts are self-hosted and do not contact external font services. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical request information, including IP addresses, for security and operation. See the GitHub privacy statement.

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date above identifies the latest revision. Contact: hello.toolsmint@gmail.com.
