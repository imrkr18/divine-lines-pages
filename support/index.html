Here to help

A little guidance.

Questions about Divine Lines, a purchase, or your privacy? Contact us directly.

hello.toolsmint@gmail.com

What to include

Tell us your device model, iOS or Android version, app version, and what happened. For a purchase issue, include the store and product name; a redacted receipt or transaction reference can help. Never send your password, payment card details, or private devotional notes.

Premium access is missing

Check that you are signed into the same Apple or Google account used for the purchase. Open the app’s purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. If access is still missing, email us with the details above.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by your store. Follow Apple’s cancellation instructions or Google Play’s subscription instructions. Uninstalling Divine Lines does not cancel your subscription.

Request a refund

Use Apple’s refund service or Google Play’s refund guidance. Eligibility is determined by the store and applicable law.

Saving a wallpaper

On iOS, saving exports an image to Photos after you grant permission. You can then set it as wallpaper using your device’s controls. Android wallpaper support depends on your device. Scripture selection intervals in the app do not mean your system wallpaper changes automatically.

Privacy requests

Email hello.toolsmint@gmail.com with your request. See our Privacy Policy for information about local app data, purchases, and support messages.
