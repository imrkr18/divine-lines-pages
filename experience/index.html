A few things to know

Clear words.
Thoughtful answers.

Does it change my wallpaper automatically?

No. In-app scripture intervals update the reading selection. They do not promise automatic background wallpaper changes. On iOS, you save an image to Photos and set your wallpaper through the device’s controls. Android wallpaper actions depend on supported device capabilities.

Do I need to create an account?

No developer account is needed to read, search, or bookmark scripture. Subscriptions use your Apple or Google store account. Our Privacy Policy explains purchase processing and local app data.

Which Bible translation is used?

Divine Lines uses the World English Bible, a public-domain English translation. Scripture includes its passage reference. Devotional commentary is separate from the quoted Bible text.

How will subscriptions work?

Premium purchases will be handled through Apple App Store or Google Play. The store confirmation screen will show pricing, billing period, and any eligible trial. Manage or cancel through your store account. See our Terms of Service.
