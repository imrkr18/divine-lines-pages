The heart behind Divine Lines

Built with intention.
Offered with faith.

We believe the smallest moments can hold something sacred. Divine Lines is our way of making a little more room for them.

Our purpose

More than something
beautiful to look at.

We reach for our phones throughout the day. Our hope is that, somewhere among those ordinary glances, a line of scripture can become an invitation to pause, pray, or begin again.

That is the heart of Divine Lines: Christian scripture presented with reverence and care. The design is there to give the words room. The words are what matter.

What guides the work

Faith in the purpose.
Care in the details.

I

Scripture at the center.

We use the World English Bible, a public-domain translation. Passages keep their references, so beauty never comes at the expense of knowing where the words come from.

II

A quieter kind of design.

Legible type. Restful color. Space around a verse. We want the experience to feel considered, without asking for more of your attention than it needs.

III

Respect for the personal.

No account is needed for reading and bookmarks. Your saved passages and preferences stay on your device. Purchase services are explained clearly in our privacy policy.
